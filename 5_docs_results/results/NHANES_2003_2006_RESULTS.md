### Reading

* **Test 1 replicates for the well-powered targets**: accelerometry adds over demographics in 2003-2006 for functional_limitation, fatigue, fair_poor_health, mortality (hip counts), as it did with wrist MIMS in 2011-2014 (the ME/CFS-like proxy, NULL in 2011-2014, is discussed below). Depressive symptoms (152 cases, one cycle) lose significance; the point estimate is positive but the CI includes 0.
* **Test 2 (over the full clinical model) stays small**: SUPPORTED in 2003-2006 for functional_limitation. Functional limitation, the only robust 2011-2014 Test 2 result, keeps CI > 0 with pen_lr and hgb but not lr, and its 2003-04 -> 2005-06 temporal Delta includes 0: the increment reproduces in direction and size (~+0.004 AUROC) but is fragile. Fatigue and depression Test 2 do not replicate (they were already not robust).
* **Accelerometry alone vs demographics**: wearable-only models are again below or equal to the 9-variable demographic model for every target except the ME/CFS-like proxy.
* **ME/CFS-like proxy (PROXY, not ME/CFS)**: with only 36 cases, the 2003-2006 increments are large but imprecise (CI width ~0.09) and the Test 2 shuffle control does not pass; the 2011-2014 NULL (132 cases) remains the better-powered estimate. Treat the 2003-2006 signal as a hypothesis, not a replication failure or success.
* **CRP adds nothing** over the clinical set for any target (all Delta AUROC within +/-0.002), and adding wearable features on top of clinical + CRP gives the same increments as without CRP.
* **Positive control passes**: mortality wearable-only AUROC well above chance and a positive increment over demographics; no increment over the full clinical model (as in 2011-2014).

## 5. Caveats

* Hip counts vs wrist MIMS: different device, placement, unit and wear protocol (waking hours only in 2003-2006, so IS/IV/M10/L5/RA describe the waking-wear profile; no sleep features).
* Fatigue, PHQ-9 >= 10 and the ME/CFS-like proxy exist only in 2005-2006 (about half the n); the proxy (a PROXY, never ME/CFS) has few cases and wide CIs; COPD was not asked, so the proxy's exclusion list is one item shorter than in 2011-2014.
* Mortality follow-up is ~6 years longer than for 2011-2014, so its prevalence and AUROCs are not directly comparable.
* Creatine kinase is absent from the 2003-2006 biochemistry profile; gout, celiac and psoriasis items are not asked (comorbidity count is built from fewer items).
* No survey weights in prediction metrics; the valid-wear subset is not re-weighted.
