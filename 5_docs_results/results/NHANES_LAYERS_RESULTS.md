## 4. What this does and does not show

* Every estimate is conditional on having the layer: subsample populations (one-third environmental subsamples, fasting, surplus sera) differ in size and composition, so Deltas are not comparable across rows.
* ALL_FULL and the subsample groups are measurement bundles, not mechanisms; an increment means better ranking of self-reported targets in this cross-sectional sample, nothing causal.
* The ME/CFS-like proxy is a PROXY (fatigue + limitation without major explanatory diagnoses), never ME/CFS; with 132 cases overall it is not estimable in most subsample populations.
* Below-LOD values are NCHS substitution values; analytes detected in < 50% of the layer were not used. Layer feature lists: `nhanes_layers_features.csv`.
* No survey weights in prediction metrics (as in the core analysis).
