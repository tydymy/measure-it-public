# Code of conduct

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.
By taking part in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) you agree to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and critique work rather than people.
Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

Two points specific to this project:

* Many people who contribute or ask questions here live with the conditions it studies (Long COVID, ME/CFS, POTS,
  fibromyalgia, hEDS/HSD, MCAS and others). Do not dismiss anyone's illness, and do not use issues to give or ask for
  individual medical advice.
* Never post anyone's health information, including your own, in a public space of this project (see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

**Reporting.** Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at **CONDUCT_CONTACT_EMAIL** (to be filled in before the
repository is made public). Reports are handled confidentially; enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
